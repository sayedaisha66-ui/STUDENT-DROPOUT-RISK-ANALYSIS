
🎓 Student Dropout Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes student dropout patterns using Power BI to identify the key factors influencing student attrition. The dashboard explores financial, academic, demographic, and wellbeing-related variables to uncover high-risk student groups and provide actionable insights for improving student retention.

🎯 Objectives
Identify the primary drivers of student dropout.
Analyze the impact of financial and demographic factors.
Evaluate how academic performance affects retention.
Investigate the influence of wellbeing and lifestyle factors.
Examine how multiple risk factors combine to increase dropout risk.
🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI
Power Query
DAX
Data Modeling
Interactive Visualizations

📊 Dashboard Sections
1. Student Profile & Financial Context

Analyzes the impact of:

Family Income
Tuition Payment Status
Disability Status
First-Generation Status
Student Demographics
2. Academic Performance Analysis

Evaluates the relationship between dropout rates and:
GPA Performance
Attendance Rate
Assignment Submission Rate
Failed Units
Stress Levels
Satisfaction Score
Sleep Hours
Commute Time

3. Combined Group Analysis

Explores the interaction between multiple risk factors through:
Family Income + Disability Status
Tuition Status + First-Generation Status
Decomposition Tree Analysis
Student Risk Segmentation

🔍 Key Insights
Low-income students exhibit the highest dropout rates.
Students with outstanding tuition payments are significantly more likely to drop out.
First-generation and disabled students face higher attrition risk.
Low attendance and poor assignment submission rates are strongly associated with dropout.
Students with lower GPAs and higher numbers of failed units experience substantially higher dropout rates.
High stress levels and low satisfaction scores negatively impact retention.
Commute time shows no strong linear relationship with dropout rates.
Students exposed to multiple risk factors simultaneously represent the most vulnerable groups.

💡 Recommendations
Implement early warning systems based on attendance, GPA, and failed units.
Provide targeted financial assistance for high-risk students.
Strengthen academic support and mentoring programs.
Improve student wellbeing and mental health initiatives.
